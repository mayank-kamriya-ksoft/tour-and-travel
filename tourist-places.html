UNESCO Heritage

Ajanta Caves

100 km from Sambhajinagar 9:00 AM - 5:30 PM (Closed Monday) ₹40 (Indian) | ₹600 (Foreign)

The Ajanta Caves are 30 rock-cut Buddhist cave monuments dating from the 2nd century BCE to about 480 CE. The caves include paintings and rock-cut sculptures described as among the finest surviving examples of ancient Indian art. They are a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most visited tourist attractions in Maharashtra.

30 Rock-Cut Caves Ancient Paintings Buddhist Sculptures UNESCO Site

Ellora Caves

30 km from Sambhajinagar 9:00 AM - 5:30 PM (Closed Tuesday) ₹40 (Indian) | ₹600 (Foreign)

Ellora Caves are a UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring 34 caves — Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain rock-cut temples built between the 6th and 11th centuries. The highlight is the magnificent Kailasa Temple (Cave 16), the largest monolithic rock excavation in the world, dedicated to Lord Shiva.

34 Caves Kailasa Temple Multi-Faith Heritage UNESCO Site

Historical Fort

Daulatabad Fort (Devagiri)

16 km from Sambhajinagar 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (All Days) ₹25 (Indian) | ₹300 (Foreign)

Daulatabad Fort, also known as Devagiri, is a 12th-century hilltop fortress considered one of the most powerful forts in India. It features an ingenious defense system with multiple layers of walls, a moat, and a dark passage. The Chand Minar, a 60-meter tower, stands as a prominent landmark.

Hilltop Fortress Chand Minar Dark Passage Panoramic Views

Mughal Heritage

Bibi Ka Maqbara

In Sambhajinagar City 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM (All Days) ₹25 (Indian) | ₹300 (Foreign)

Known as the "Taj of the Deccan," Bibi Ka Maqbara was built by Prince Azam Shah in 1678 as a tribute to his mother Dilras Banu Begum. The monument bears a striking resemblance to the Taj Mahal and is surrounded by beautiful Mughal gardens with fountains and pathways.

Panchakki (Water Mill)

In Sambhajinagar City 8:00 AM - 9:00 PM (All Days) ₹10

Panchakki is a 17th-century water mill that demonstrates the engineering marvel of medieval India. The mill was used to grind grain for pilgrims. The complex includes a beautiful garden, a fish pond, and the tomb of Sufi saint Baba Shah Musafir. The underground water channel system is fascinating.

Water Mill Sufi Shrine Garden & Pond Engineering Marvel

Jyotirlinga

Grishneshwar Temple

30 km from Sambhajinagar (Near Ellora) 5:30 AM - 9:30 PM (All Days) Free Entry

Grishneshwar Temple is one of the 12 Jyotirlinga shrines sacred to Lord Shiva. Located near Ellora Caves, this ancient temple features beautiful red stone architecture with intricate carvings. It is believed to be the last of the 12 Jyotirlingas and attracts devotees from across India.

Buddhist Heritage

Aurangabad Caves

5 km from Sambhajinagar City 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Closed Monday) ₹25 (Indian) | ₹300 (Foreign)

The Aurangabad Caves are 12 Buddhist rock-cut shrines dating from the 2nd to 6th century CE. Located on a hillside, these caves feature beautiful sculptures, particularly of women in various poses. They are less crowded than Ajanta and Ellora, offering a peaceful exploration experience.

Salim Ali Lake & Bird Sanctuary

In Sambhajinagar City 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM ₹10

Named after the renowned ornithologist Dr. Salim Ali, this lake and bird sanctuary is a haven for birdwatchers. The sanctuary hosts numerous migratory and local bird species. It's a peaceful escape within the city, perfect for nature walks and photography.
