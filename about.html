Our Story

PandaCabs Tours & Travels

Founded in 2016, PandaCabs Tours & Travels has been serving the people of Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) with reliable and affordable travel solutions. What started as a small taxi service has grown into a trusted name for tourist place visits, outstation travel, and airport transfers.

We understand the rich heritage of our city — from the UNESCO World Heritage sites of Ajanta and Ellora to the magnificent Bibi Ka Maqbara. Our experienced drivers double as knowledgeable guides who can share the history and stories behind every monument.

Whether you need a one-way taxi to Mumbai, a round trip to Pune, or a comfortable airport pickup, we ensure a safe, clean, and on-time experience every single time.
