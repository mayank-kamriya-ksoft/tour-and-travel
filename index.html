Quick answers about our taxi services & tourist places

The one-way taxi fare from Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) to Mumbai starts at ₹3,500 for a sedan (Swift Dzire/Etios). SUV rates start at ₹5,000. Round trip fares start at ₹6,500 for sedan. The distance is approximately 330 km and takes about 6 hours via NH-52. All fares include fuel, toll, and driver allowance.

One-way taxi from Sambhajinagar to Pune costs ₹2,800 for a sedan. SUV rates start at ₹4,000. Round trip starts at ₹5,200. The distance is 235 km, taking approximately 4.5 hours via NH-52. Price includes toll, fuel, and driver charges.

Airport pickup or drop at Sambhajinagar (Chikkalthana) Airport costs ₹500 for a sedan and ₹800 for an SUV. Round trip (pickup + drop) is ₹900 for sedan and ₹1,400 for SUV. We offer free 30-minute waiting time and flight tracking.

The top tourist places near Sambhajinagar include: Ajanta Caves (100 km, UNESCO World Heritage), Ellora Caves (30 km, UNESCO site with Kailasa Temple), Daulatabad Fort (16 km), Bibi Ka Maqbara (in city, Taj of Deccan), Panchakki (in city), Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga (30 km), and Salim Ali Lake.

We provide both one-way and round trip taxi services. One-way is ideal when you don't need a return ride. Round trip is better value if you need the car for both directions. We also offer multi-day packages for outstation travel.

We offer: Sedan (Swift Dzire, Toyota Etios) for 4 passengers, SUV (Ertiga, Innova) for 6-7 passengers, Premium (Innova Crysta) for 6-7 passengers, and Tempo Traveller (12-17 seater) for groups. All vehicles are AC, clean, and well-maintained.
